# Revision: Final Review

You have the initial review (below) and a critique of that review (below).
Your task: produce the **final** review by applying every fix from the
critique.

## Rules of revision

1. **Address every blocker and major issue from the critique.** If you
   cannot address one (e.g., because the critique was wrong), state that
   explicitly in the revision notes at the end and explain why.
2. **Preserve the review's overall structure.** Follow the same section
   order as `synthesis_prompt.md`.
3. **Fix in place; do not append.** If the critique flagged an unsupported
   claim, rewrite that claim to be supported (or delete it), do not add a
   caveat next to it.
4. **Re-check citations.** Every citation that was flagged as hallucinated
   or incorrect must either be corrected (with a real source you can verify
   in the paper or via a fresh check) or removed.
5. **Recalibrate the verdict** if the critique revealed that
   Strengths/Weaknesses were misweighted.
6. **Do not add NEW material** beyond what was needed to fix the critique.
   Scope creep is a failure mode — the goal is a corrected review, not a
   bigger one.

## Paper grounding

The original paper is still available (PDF + Markdown). Use it to verify any
claim you adjust. When the critique says "the review claims X but §3.2 says
Y," actually go read §3.2 and decide which is right — then fix the review
accordingly. Do not blindly defer to the critique; the critique can also be
wrong.

## Output

Produce the full revised review, ready for publication to the authors / area
chair. At the very end, add a small section:

```
## Revision Notes (for editor only — strip before sending to authors)
- <issue 1>: fixed by <change>
- <issue 2>: not fixed because <reason>
- <issue 3>: fixed by <change>
```

This trailing section is for the editor/human-in-the-loop and will be
stripped by the critic check if needed.

Produce the final review now.
